Mobile App Development

Edutech Mobile

An interactive learning platform connecting rural students with expert educators across the country.

The Challenge

Access to quality education remains a significant challenge for students in remote rural areas. A lack of expert teachers and educational resources severely limits academic potential. Existing solutions were often desktop-focused, requiring reliable broadband internet which is scarce in these regions.

We needed to build a mobile-first platform that could operate efficiently on low-bandwidth networks, delivering interactive video lessons and real-time assessments directly to smartphones.

The Solution

We engineered a cross-platform mobile application optimized for low latency. The app features offline-sync capabilities, allowing students to download video lectures and interactive quizzes when connected, and complete them entirely offline.

The interface was designed to be highly engaging, utilizing gamification elements like progress tracking, achievement badges, and peer-to-peer discussion boards to foster a sense of community among remote learners.

Technologies Used:

Flutter Dart Firebase Authentication GraphQL AWS S3 (Video Hosting)

The Impact

Since launch, Edutech Mobile has successfully onboarded over 50,000 active students from rural districts. Average test scores among users have seen a 25% improvement, and the platform has democratized access to expert instruction that was previously out of reach.
