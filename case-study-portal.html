Full-Stack Web App

Citizen Portal

Streamlining public service requests and grievance handling with an intuitive, citizen-first interface.

The Challenge

Public service requests and grievance reporting were highly bureaucratic, requiring citizens to visit government offices in person, fill out paper forms, and wait weeks for a resolution. The lack of a centralized tracking system led to lost documents, frustrated citizens, and zero accountability for service delays.

The municipality needed a modern, secure, and user-friendly web application that allowed citizens to submit, track, and resolve their grievances entirely online.

The Solution

We developed a full-stack web application designed with a citizen-first philosophy. The frontend was built to be highly accessible and responsive, ensuring that citizens could easily use the portal from mobile browsers without downloading an app.

On the backend, we implemented a sophisticated ticketing workflow that automatically routed grievances to the appropriate municipal department based on predefined categories. We integrated SMS and email notifications to keep citizens updated in real-time regarding the status of their requests.

Technologies Used:

Vue.js Laravel (PHP) MySQL Twilio API (SMS) Redis Caching

The Impact

The Citizen Portal digitized over 150 unique municipal services. Within the first six months, the average resolution time for public grievances dropped from 21 days to just 4 days. The system processed over 10,000 successful requests, significantly boosting public satisfaction and reducing the administrative burden on government staff.
